experiments are named

    <revision>-<set>-<cost>-<algos>-<repetitions>

where

- <revision> is
    - 'base' for the base implementation
    - 'nocachew' for the variant that computes weights on the fly
    - 'expfix' for nocachew with fixed std::exp

- <set> is
    - 'satmost' for SUITE_SATISFICING of project.py
    - 'sat1114' for roughly ipc sat 11 and 14

- <cost> is
    - 'u' for unit costs
    - 'g' for costs as defined by the problems

- <algos> is
    - 'all' for all

- <repetitions> is the number of times stochastic algorithms are repeated
